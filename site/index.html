Never quietly wrong

Prices refresh every minute. If an update fails, the app says so and the tray price gets a ⚠. It won't show an old price as if it were new.

Tiny and private

Native Rust and Tauri, with no Electron. There is no account and no tracking. The only server it talks to is CoinGecko.

        Questions

        Is it really free?

        Yes. It has no ads, no subscription and no account, and the source is on GitHub.

        Where do the prices come from?

        From CoinGecko's public market data, refreshed every 60 seconds. If CoinGecko rate-limits or can't be reached, the app shows how old its last price is.

        Does it collect any data?

        No. Your coin and currency choices stay on your computer. The only network requests go to CoinGecko for prices and coin icons.

        Why does my system warn me when I open it?

        The builds aren't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate. The steps under each download get you past the warning. If you'd rather not trust a binary, you can build it from source.

        How do I start it at login?

        On macOS, add it under System Settings → General → Login Items. On Windows, put a shortcut in shell:startup. On Linux, use your desktop's autostart settings.
